Your files stay on your Mac.

Local by default. Private by design. Every conversion runs locally on your machine using Apple's native frameworks and a bundled, self-contained ffmpeg. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server or external service.

App source: GPL-3.0. Bundled ffmpeg: GPL (libx264/libx265). WebP & JPEG XL tools: BSD.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tossy free?

Yes. It is free and open source under GPL-3.0. No accounts, no subscriptions, and no ads.

Does Tossy upload my files anywhere?

No. Every operation executes entirely offline on your Mac. No data ever leaves your device.

Do I need to install Homebrew or ffmpeg?

No. ffmpeg, WebP, and JPEG XL reference binaries are bundled directly inside Tossy and run standalone.

What formats are supported?

Over 60 formats across images, video, and audio: HEIC, RAW camera files, MP4, MKV, WebM, FLAC, Animated GIFs, and Animated WebP.
